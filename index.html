Meet Seva

Real listening. Honest work. Lasting movement.

My path began with psychology, NLP, hypnotherapy and Reverse Therapy. I worked with high-performing people, then chose to take that experience into places where the stakes were different: supporting children, people experiencing homelessness and people living with addiction.

That work taught me that technique only matters when a person feels genuinely seen. I learned to listen without judgement, stay present with complexity and help people find movement when they feel most lost.

Today I bring more than two decades of learning and practice into an approach that is intuitive, practical and shaped around the individual. The aim is not to make you dependent on therapy. It is to help you understand yourself, trust yourself and create change you can carry into everyday life.

Psychology

Psychology degree from London South Bank University, with a focus on child cognitive development.

Specialist training

Ericksonian approaches, NLP and Positive Psychology, including training with Stephen Gilligan, Bill O’Hanlon, Richard Bandler and Paul McKenna.

Frontline experience

Five years supporting people through Change Grow Live, with extensive experience of addiction recovery and complex lives.
